# Nearby play: the Storyteller's phone holds the grimoire and sends each player only their own

Status: accepted 2026-10-20 (GMC-1658, after Werewolf's nearby moderator, GMC-1657)

Blood on the Clocktower has to work with no internet: phones in one room on one Wi-Fi or one phone's hotspot, one of them the Storyteller's. It reuses Werewolf's split (`../../werewolf/docs/adr/0003-nearby-moderator-private-payloads.md`) on the shared LAN room (`../../common/lan/room.js`), with no change to `common/`.

Decision: only the public half of the game is ever broadcast.

- **Room (broadcast).** The script (which characters could be in play is public at a real table), seats in circle order, alive/dead, each dead player's ghost vote, phase and day, today's nominations, the open vote's nominator and nominee, each closed vote's yes-voters (hands are public in BotC), who is on the block, the public log, and every true character (and what each player thought they were) only after the game is over.
- **Grimoire (the Storyteller's phone only, in memory).** Each player's true character and the character they are told (`thinks`: the Drunk, the Marionette and the Lunatic are told someone else), the three Demon bluffs, reminder tokens, the asks sent, every answer, the evil team's first-night info as sent, and the notes sent to each player.
- **Private payloads.** `privateFor(room, secret, clientId)`, sent with `hub.setPrivate` and resent on reconnect: the character they are told (with its ability), their evil-team info if the Storyteller has sent it (Minions: the Demon and the other Minions; the Demon: the Minions and the bluffs), the questions open for them now, their own answers, and their notes. Reminder tokens never leave the grimoire.
- **Answers.** `peer.send({ t: 'act', id, choice })`, with `choice` `{ players?, character?, yes? }`. The Storyteller recomputes the player's open prompts and refuses stale or invalid answers (closed question, wrong count, a player or character that is not a choice).

What the app does, and what it leaves to the Storyteller:

- **Setup.** Script: Trouble Brewing, Bad Moon Rising, Sects & Violets (from `resources/data/roles.json` by edition), or a pasted script in `script-schema.json` form; only released characters are used (homebrew, Travellers and Fabled are skipped and listed). The bag is picked by hand or suggested at the rulebook's counts (5-15 players); setup characters (Baron, Drunk...) are flagged and their adjustment is the Storyteller's. Deal is random; the Drunk and Marionette default to a not-in-play Townsfolk on the script and the Lunatic to a Demon on the script; bluffs default to three not-in-play good characters. All of it is editable in the grimoire, also mid-game (an Imp's star-pass, a Pit-Hag: the player is told the new character).
- **Night.** The Storyteller sees tonight's steps in upstream's night order (`nightsheet.json`, first night vs other nights) for the characters in play, waking each player as the character they think they are; minion info and demon info appear on the first night with 7 or more players (the rulebook's threshold) with a Send button. Each step's question is read from its reminder text ("chooses 2 players", "a living player", "a player & a character", "(not yourself)" in the ability) and sent with Ask, or to every chooser at once (skipping the dead and conditional steps such as "If the Ravenkeeper died tonight"). Phones show the question with the character's ability, not the night-sheet text. The Storyteller can also ask any player anything (0-3 players, a character, yes/no). Asks close when the phase changes.
- **Information.** The app does not resolve any ability. What a player learns is the Storyteller's call, sent as a free-text note (with a character-name helper), exactly as at a table where the Storyteller decides what to show.
- **Day.** A living player who has not nominated today can ask to nominate from their phone; the Storyteller opens the vote (or opens one directly). Rules enforced: one nomination per living player per day, each player nominated once a day, one open vote at a time. The living and the dead with a ghost vote vote yes/no on their phones; ballots are hidden until the vote closes, then the yes-voters are public, dead yes-voters lose their ghost vote, and the block follows the rulebook (half the living rounded up; beating today's best puts the nominee on the block, a tie frees it). The Storyteller executes (the block, or anyone) and starts the night. Butler, Virgin, Slayer and similar effects are the Storyteller's to apply (Kill/Revive, ghost vote toggle).
- **End.** The Storyteller picks the winner; every phone then shows every true character and what each player thought they were.

Not done: Travellers and Fabled, homebrew characters, automatic jinx/setup application, Continue as host (GMC-2054). Limits are the same as Werewolf's: the clientId is not authenticated, and if the Storyteller's page closes the game ends.

Code: `lan/rules.js` (pure; `test/lan.rules.test.js` on the real character data), `lan/storyteller.js` (`NearbyStoryteller` on a hub; `test/lan.storyteller.test.js` plays a 7-player game over in-memory connections and checks each phone's wire for leaks), `nearby.njk` (the page, at /games/botc/nearby/).
